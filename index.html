Your truck

If your truck is governed (for example at 65 mph), enter it. Arrival times are worked out as if you never go faster than this, even where the posted limit is higher. Leave blank for none.

Route preferences

"Avoid" is a strong preference, not a ban: if there is no other way, a route may still use one. Press Find route again after changing these. Routes show whether they use tolls or a ferry, but toll prices are not available yet.
